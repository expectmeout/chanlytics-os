# Glossary

The product's words, what each one is in storage, and where the two do not
yet agree. Read this before the code: most of the repository's names make
sense only against it.

## One rule about tenant and workspace

Storage says **tenant**; people and the API say **workspace**. They are the
same thing: one business's account. `tenants`, `tenant_id`, `tenant_channels`
and `tenant_config` are the storage names and stay that way for consistency;
`?workspace=`, `/w/<slug>`, `create_workspace` and every sentence a person
reads say workspace. Never mix the two inside one layer: a new column is
`tenant_id`, a new route parameter is `workspace`.

## One rule about person and contact

Someone the business talks to is a **person**. Storage says **contact** only
in the names it already has: the tables `contacts` and `contact_identities`,
the functions that already carry the word (`set_contact_consent`,
`lead_link_contact`), and every foreign key to `contacts`, which is named for
the table it references (`contact_id`, a function's `p_contact_id`). Those
stay. Every new name says person, SQL included: a table, a column that is not
a foreign key, a function, a route, a type, a word on a page (`person_notes`,
`create_person`, `set_person_proposal`, `PersonNoteDto`, People). A new route
segment is never an old `contacts/` path extended; the API's `contacts/`
routes are People's row below, where the names do not agree yet.

Contact keeps one other meaning, in its own scope: a named person at a
business who is not one of the workspace's people. Today that is the business
record's contact, who signs the carriers' registration for the workspace's own
business (`business_records.contact_first_name` to `contact_job_position`, the
carriers' word); someone the finder names at a lead's business would be the
same kind of contact. Such a contact is no person until they write in, or, at
a lead's business, a reach-out makes one (`reach_out_person`). To contact is a
verb: a lead's `contacted_at`, a search's `contact` (phone or email).

## Where the names do not agree yet

These are known and deliberate for now. Storage keeps its words for good (the
two rules above). An old noun in the API stays until a change moves it with
its callers, the old path kept for a release; a hash route leaves with the
shell (decision 0015). A new route or page never extends an old noun.

## Words to avoid

- **Salon, stylist, visit** as product words. The first workspace was a salon
  and the code still uses it as the worked example in comments; the product is
  for any business that turns a conversation into a booked time.
  `tooling/scripts/tests/repo/trade-neutral.test.mjs` fails a build that puts one
  trade's words in front of a customer of another.
- **Bot, agent** for the receptionist. "Agent" is reserved for the deferred
  agent-platform surfaces of the mockup; the thing that answers is the
  receptionist.
- **Client** for a person. It collides with the Supabase client, the HTTP
  client and the typed API client, and the shell's `#/clients` route is the
  last place it survives.
