# Glossary

The product's words, what each one is in storage, and where the two do not
yet agree. Read this before the code: most of the repository's names make
sense only against it.

## One rule about tenant and workspace

Storage says **tenant**; people and the API say **workspace**. They are the
same thing: one business's account. `tenants`, `tenant_id`, `tenant_channels`
and `tenant_config` are the storage names and stay that way for consistency;
`?workspace=`, `/w/<slug>`, `create_workspace` and every sentence a person
reads say workspace. Never mix the two inside one layer: a new column is
`tenant_id`, a new route parameter is `workspace`.

## The words

| Word                             | What it is                                                                                                                       | In storage                                                                                                                                                                      | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Workspace**                    | One business's account, its people, its channels and its receptionist                                                            | `tenants`                                                                                                                                                                       | Timezone lives here and decides what "today" means everywhere                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Member**                       | A signed-in person who works in a workspace, with a role: owner, operator, partner or viewer                                     | `memberships`, `profiles`, `invitations`                                                                                                                                        | Owners change settings and money; operators approve and act; viewers read. A **partner** (0012) helps run it: settings, channels and the finder, never billing or the team. Team hands out the other three; a partner comes by their link, a handover or the owner's ask (0020)    |
| **Person**                       | Someone the business talks to: a customer, a caller, a prospect who wrote in                                                     | `contacts`, plus one `contact_identities` row per channel identity                                                                                                              | Keyed by phone today, with an email since 00125. Shown as **People**; the API still says `contacts`                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Lead**                         | A business or person the finder found, not yet a person the workspace talks to                                                   | `leads`, `lead_searches`, `lead_rounds`, `lead_runs`, `lead_places`, `lead_search_members`, `lead_suppressions`                                                                 | Kept apart from `contacts` on purpose: nothing that sends can reach a lead but a reach-out, which makes its person first (`reach_out_person`). A lead is linked to a person the moment they write in (`lead_link_contact`), and that person's consent can only ever come from them |
| **Conversation** (thread)        | One per person per workspace, for ever, across every channel                                                                     | `conversations`                                                                                                                                                                 | Its state (`open`, `needs_human`, `snoozed`, `archived`), who holds it, who is speaking now, and until when                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Message**                      | One thing said, either direction, on one channel                                                                                 | `messages` with `channel` in `whatsapp`, `voice`, `sms`, `email`, `system`                                                                                                      | A queued outbound row with a delay is a **draft** the receptionist is holding                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Call**                         | A phone conversation, its transcript, recording and outcome                                                                      | `calls`, linked to the conversation                                                                                                                                             | Voice is a channel, but a call is its own record, drawn in the thread                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Channel**                      | A way a customer reaches the business, as a routed row                                                                           | `tenant_channels.kind` in `whatsapp`, `voice`, `sms`, `email`, `instagram`                                                                                                      | The row is the routing key a webhook resolves; its credentials live in Vault                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Line**                         | A phone number the platform provisioned, with its carrier registration                                                           | `lines`                                                                                                                                                                         | Twilio subaccount, number, Messaging Service, A2P state                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Mailbox**                      | The workspace's email address, on the hosted mail domain                                                                         | `mailboxes`                                                                                                                                                                     | Made once by an owner; the reply route's token never leaves the database. Cold email's own domains are sending domains                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Sending domain**               | A workspace's own domain that cold email leaves from, apart from its mailbox                                                     | `sending_domains` (00175), five per workspace                                                                                                                                   | Verified at the cold mail account; a hold for its records lifts itself, one for bounces, a complaint or unsubscribes waits for Resume, and a second such hold in 30 days for the platform (00188)                                                                                  |
| **Reach-out**                    | Cold email to the businesses a lead search found: a chain of steps, the first email and the nudges after it                      | `playbooks` rows of `kind = 'reach_out'`: the root (`lead_first`) and its steps (`after_step`)                                                                                  | Each step runs as a `broadcasts` row, a run per day, its first waiting for its owner                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Send**                         | One lead in one step's run: planned, checked, claimed, queued, then sent; or held for its owner, skipped or failed, with why     | `reach_out_sends` (00183, 00197)                                                                                                                                                | Its email is a queued `messages` row whose id is derived from the send and the emails written for it (`attempts`)                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Check**                        | What every reach-out email passes before it may go: the code rules, and for words that name `{{opening}}`, a writer and a reader | `reach_out_sends.checked_at`, `checked_words`, `check_gen` (00197); its own clock, `reach-out-check-sweep`                                                                      | The claim takes only a checked send. New words, or a new name to sign with, send it back to be checked                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Opening**                      | The one sentence the check writes for each business, from its record, before the owner's own words                               | `reach_out_sends.opening`, with `facts_used`                                                                                                                                    | Named in the words as `{{opening}}`. Every number and named fact in it must be in the record; one rewrite, then held                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Angle**                        | The few words that say what an opening leaned on ("their rating")                                                                | `reach_out_sends.angle`                                                                                                                                                         | At most three words. Reports by angle are Part 6's                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Held** (a send)                | A reach-out email the check stopped for its owner's say, with the reason and the doors                                           | `reach_out_sends.state = 'held'`, `reason`, `hold_detail`; answered by `reach_out_send_decide`                                                                                  | Reasons: a customer's domain, a chain, another language, an unbacked fact, reads like spam, or the model could not answer (`check_unavailable`, checked again an hour on). Include, Rewrite, Skip or Not a fit; unanswered for fourteen days it is given up. It keeps its run open |
| **Reading** (of a reply)         | What a reply to a reach-out was read as, before anyone answers it, and what that did                                             | `reach_out_replies` (00222), one row per message; made by the `reply_read` job                                                                                                  | Read by Haiku, applied once through `reach_out_reply_apply` by contracts' `readingPlan`. A reply that could not be read is `other`, with why (`unread`), and is read again up to three times in seven days. A no can be undone for seven days                                      |
| **Intent**                       | What a reply meant: interested, a question, not now, no, wrong person, away, or something else                                   | `reach_out_replies.intent`; the words are contracts' `REPLY_INTENT_WORDS`                                                                                                       | Only the intent decides whether there is a reading. Each one's effects are one table, `readingPlan`: Talking, a dated step, Said no, or nothing but the stop                                                                                                                       |
| **Away** (an automatic reply's)  | An out-of-office read as away: the chain pauses until the day it names, or seven days                                            | `reach_out_replies.until_at`, where `claim = 'auto_reply'`; the door's fact `away`, the refusal `outreach_away`                                                                 | A skip, not a wait. A person who writes "I'm away" stops the chain like any reply, with a dated step for their return (N1)                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Dated step**                   | A step for the team on a person's page, due on a day, that the product writes, not a member                                      | `person_notes` rows from `reach_out` with `due_on`, written by `person_step_from_playbook` (00220), once per source                                                             | Shows under Steps due on Today. Made by a not-now, a wrong person, an away in their own words, and a call or a note for you                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Handed** (a hand step's send)  | A lead a chain's last step gave to the team as a call or a note, rather than an email                                            | `reach_out_sends.state = 'handed'` (00228); the step is `playbooks.hand`                                                                                                        | Sends nothing and never moves. The chain's report counts it apart from written, and Playbooks says "N handed to you"                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Receptionist**                 | The AI that answers on every channel                                                                                             | `tenant_config.brain` (the prompt, the business facts, the fallbacks); `policies` (the Autonomy matrix)                                                                         | The code that talks is `apps/edge/lib/receptionist/brain.ts`; the code that decides whether to talk at all is `packages/core/src/inbound/reply-posture.ts`                                                                                                                         |
| **Autonomy**                     | How much the receptionist may do on its own, per decision class                                                                  | `policies`: `auto`, `veto`, `ask`, `never`                                                                                                                                      | Money is locked to `never` by a trigger. The UI shows each mode in words, never the stored value                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Escalation**                   | A thread waiting for a person, and why                                                                                           | `escalations`, one open row per thread                                                                                                                                          | Resolved by a person or by the thread moving on                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Decision**                     | An operator verb on a thread: reply, take over, hand back, snooze, wake, resolve, archive, approve, steer                        | `decisions`                                                                                                                                                                     | The ledger of what people did; `events` is the ledger of what happened                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Consent**                      | Two answers per person, kept apart because the carriers require it: conversational, and marketing                                | `contacts.consent`, `contacts.marketing_consent`, with the evidence on `events`                                                                                                 | One predicate decides every send: `mayMessageFor`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Template**                     | A message shape a provider approved (Meta) or the workspace keeps, with slots                                                    | `templates`                                                                                                                                                                     | Its purpose comes from the provider's category and fails closed to marketing                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Broadcast**                    | One approved template to an audience, once                                                                                       | `broadcasts`, `broadcast_recipients`                                                                                                                                            | Audience crossed with consent at enrolment and again at every send                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Playbook**                     | A standing rule that sends one template to people who newly qualify                                                              | `playbooks`                                                                                                                                                                     | A run is a broadcast; the trigger's class decides whether it sends, waits, or asks                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **List**                         | A saved set of people, kept by hand or computed                                                                                  | `segments`, `segment_members`                                                                                                                                                   | Shown as **Lists**; the API still says `segments`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Appointment** (visit, booking) | A time on the owner's calendar for a person                                                                                      | No table. Read from the owner's calendar feed at read time                                                                                                                      | The receptionist never books; the booking tool behind the link does. `docs/decisions/0002-appointments-record.md` proposes the record                                                                                                                                              |
| **Deal**                         | An engagement a person is deciding on or has taken: a proposal out, a client, passed, ended                                      | `deals` (00156): one row per engagement, its stage (`proposed`, `won`, `lost`, `ended`) and value, and since 00160 its proposal link, the day it stands until and why it passed | Written by a person through one door, `deal_record`, never by the receptionist (`docs/decisions/0004-deals-record.md`, `0008`). Only where the workspace sells by proposal (`tenants.sells_by`, `dealsAreOn`). "Client" is a stage word, never a word for a person                 |
| **Job**                          | Something that happens later, on the ledger: a send, a broadcast batch, a line's provisioning, an email intake, a lead round     | `jobs` with `kind` in `JOB_KINDS`                                                                                                                                               | Claimed with a lease, resumed by the heartbeat; a kind arrives with its handler, its caller and the CHECK together                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Sweep**                        | A clock: pg_cron calling an edge cron door with the sweep key                                                                    | `sweep_status` registers every clock                                                                                                                                            | A sweep that stops is a stale row, never a silence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Door**                         | An HTTP endpoint that proves its caller before it reads anything                                                                 | Webhooks (provider signatures), cron doors (`x-cron-key`), admin doors (`x-edge-token`), the app plane (session cookie)                                                         | The four trust boundaries in `docs/architecture.md`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Setup**                        | The rows that say what the workspace has connected, each one a fact the workspace holds                                          | Nothing stores "done": `contracts/setup/setup.ts` reads the facts                                                                                                               | A row repaints when its fact changes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Ask**                          | The assistant over the workspace's own data, from the palette                                                                    | `ask_threads`, `ask_messages`                                                                                                                                                   | Reads under the member's own RLS; acts only through a card the member approves                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Partner**                      | A member who brought the workspace, or joined it at its owner's ask, and helps run it, for a share of what it pays (0012, 0020)  | `partners` (one per account: the code, the share), `partner_tenants` (one per workspace, for good; `origin`: `link`, `handover` or `invite`), `partner_invitations`             | The link is `/p/<code>`; the cookie it leaves is read once, by `create_workspace`. The share is the row's, never a constant. `partner_earnings` is what it earned, written only by the Stripe webhook; held 30 days, then payable once the workspace qualifies                     |
| **Back office**                  | Platform staff's view of every workspace                                                                                         | `platform_admins`, `operator_pages`                                                                                                                                             | Read only through `is_platform_admin()`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Edge**                         | `apps/edge`: the webhooks, the sweeps, the job runner and the admin doors                                                        | Vercel project `chanlytics-messaging`                                                                                                                                           | Node serverless functions, not the Vercel Edge runtime. Holds every provider key                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Dashboard**                    | `apps/dashboard`: the operator UI and the `/api/v1` app plane                                                                    | Vercel project `chanlytics-os`, `app.chanlytics.com`                                                                                                                            | Holds no provider key                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## Where the names do not agree yet

These are known and deliberate for now. Each is one rename when the shell
leaves hash routes (see `docs/architecture.md`, "The shell").

| The product says | The API says        | The shell's route says |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------- |
| People           | `/api/v1/contacts`  | `#/clients`            |
| Lists            | `/api/v1/segments`  | `#/segments`           |
| Playbooks        | `/api/v1/playbooks` | `#/outbound`           |
| Templates        | `/api/v1/templates` | `#/messages`           |
| Settings         | `/api/v1/settings`  | `#/admin`              |

## Words to avoid

- **Salon, stylist, visit** as product words. The first workspace was a salon
  and the code still uses it as the worked example in comments; the product is
  for any business that turns a conversation into a booked time.
  `tooling/scripts/tests/repo/trade-neutral.test.mjs` fails a build that puts one
  trade's words in front of a customer of another.
- **Bot, agent** for the receptionist. "Agent" is reserved for the deferred
  agent-platform surfaces of the mockup; the thing that answers is the
  receptionist.
- **Client** for a person. It collides with the Supabase client, the HTTP
  client and the typed API client, and the shell's `#/clients` route is the
  last place it survives.
