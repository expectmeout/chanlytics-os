# Ask answers a search: design spec

**Date:** 24 Sep 2026 · **Owner:** Ruben · **Status:** approved by Ruben, 24 Sep ("continue as you suggest", on the closing line of #998: "Phase 2 of the finder: server-side amends so a search's questions can be answered from anywhere, and a 'search waiting on you' pill in Today") · **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-24-ask-answers-a-search.md` (one plan, one PR)
**Base:** written against `origin/main` at `11584033` (#998). Phase 1 (`2026-09-24-ask-is-the-finder-design.md`, F1–F12, built as #991/#995) stands underneath: the Leads surface, the door, the card on the rail, the plan panel. The lead finder engine (`docs/decisions/`, release B; `lead_round_answer` in 00140) is unchanged. Where this spec and the code disagree, the code wins.

## 1. Goal

A search that stopped to ask its question ("Should I include the gyms too?") waits for the owner. Today the owner can only answer it on the Leads page, in the plan panel's answer row, and that row does its own arithmetic: it sends one Ask turn with a hand-written prefix, takes the card that comes back, and posts it as `version + 1` at the round's count from the page (`leadFinderApprove`). Ask itself cannot answer: a card it proposes is always a new search, so "yes, include them" said on Today would start a second search while the first waited a day and died. And nothing outside Leads says a search is waiting at all: Today's queue, Ask's Right now row and its pills know people and playbook runs, not searches.

The goal, in one line: **a proposal can say which search it amends, the commit door answers that search, and a search waiting on its owner is in the queue, the sentence and the pills like anyone else waiting.**

What the reading found underneath (recorded so the plan's tasks make sense):

- `lead_round_answer` (00140) takes `include` only with a plan whose `version` is the round's `plan_version + 1`; the target is raised to the plan's `count`. The route (`lead-searches/[id]/answer`) passes the plan through as sent; the client computes the version. That arithmetic belongs to the server, once.
- `list_lead_searches` (the model's read) says each search's state in words but not its question, so the model cannot know what a waiting search is asking.
- `proposeLeadSearch` builds a plan from the model's words and checks it with `planLeadSearch`; it never reads a search row. `commit.ts`'s `lead_search` case posts to `POST lead-searches` and returns `{search_id}` as its undo.
- `toTodayVM(metrics, conversations, escalations, calls, now)` builds the queue from escalations, closing windows and calls; `waitingFrom` (palette.ts) turns its rows into `AskWaiting.people` and the broadcasts' pending runs into `.runs`; `rightNowLine`/`rightNowCounts`/`needsAnswer` speak those two populations. `leadsStanding` already knows "1 search needs you" on the Leads page alone.
- `GET lead-searches` is readable by every member (`read({scope: workspace})`); answering is owner-only (the route and the strip's gates).

## 2. The design, G1–G10

- **G1. One derivation of an answer's plan.** `server/leads.ts` exports `answerPlanOf(search: SearchViewRow, plan: LeadSearchPlan): LeadSearchPlan`: the plan with `version: (search.plan_version ?? 1) + 1` and `count: (search.plan as LeadSearchPlan).count` (the count the round reserved), everything else as given. The answer route applies it to every `include` before the rpc, so a client may send a plan at any version and any count and the door still takes it. `leadFinderApprove` and `leadAnswerPlan` lose their own version and count arithmetic (`baseVersion`, `count` leave `answerOf(s)`).
- **G2. The proposal says which search it amends.** `propose_lead_search` gains an optional `amends` input: the id of the search whose question the plan answers (from `list_lead_searches`). With it, `proposeLeadSearch` reads the search (`searchRow`); a search that is not `waiting_on_owner` is a refusal in words ("that search is not waiting for an answer; propose a new search instead"), and so is one the workspace does not have. The plan built from the words goes through `answerPlanOf`, and the payload carries `amends: { search_id, name, question }` beside `plan`, `view`, `facts`; the card's `undo` is `null` (an answer carries the round on; Stop is the strip's). The title stays the plan's; the summary is `Answers “{question}”: {planSentence}`.
- **G3. The model knows which search asks what.** `list_lead_searches` says, per search, `question: sentence | null` (the round's question while it waits) and its note says a search with a question is waiting for the owner's answer and is answered by `propose_lead_search` with `amends: its id`, carrying the same plan with the answer applied. The prompt's Leads sentence (the general one at `turnSystem` and the Leads-surface one) says the same in one clause; on the Leads surface with a page scope, "this search" means the search the scope names. Keep and Stop stay one press on the strip: the prompt says so ("to carry on unchanged or stop it, the owner does that on Leads").
- **G4. The commit door answers.** `commit.ts`'s `lead_search` case: with `payload.amends`, it posts `POST lead-searches/[id]/answer` with `{answer: "include", plan}` (the plan the owner approved on the card; the route sets its version and count, G1), and returns the receipt "Answered. {name} carries on with your answer; Leads shows it." with `undo: null`. Without `amends`, as today. `undoProposal` is unchanged (an answered card has no undo to offer). `cardOpenRoute` answers `leadQuestionRoute(amends.search_id)` for a card with `amends` (the search, on the pile it asked about), before the outcome's `search_id`. `AskCard` navigates after a successful approve whenever `cardOpenRoute` answers for a `lead_search` card, not only when the undo carries a `search_id`.
- **G5. The page's answer row goes through the same door.** The plan panel keeps its answer row (F5). Its turn (`leadAnswerPlan`) still sends `leadFinderAmendAsk` on the Leads surface, and the prefix now names the search's id and asks for `propose_lead_search` with `amends` set to it. The card that comes back carries `amends`, so `leadFinderApprove` commits it through `window.__ask.commit` as any card (the answering branch that called `__leadSearchAnswer` with its own version goes; the proposal branch is the one path). A card without `amends` for an answer (the model proposed a new search) is refused under the input: "That would start a new search. Say your answer to the question instead." The strip's Include (no Ask turn: `planVersionOf` takes the exclusion out and the card is opened on the page) still goes through `__leadSearchAnswer(id, "include", plan)`; the route sets the version (G1).
- **G6. A search waiting on you is in Today's queue.** `toTodayVM` gains a sixth argument, `searches: LeadSearchDto[] | null = null` (null: not read, or not fetched; the sample and every caller that does not pass it are unchanged). Each search whose round is `waiting_on_owner` is a queue row of kind `"search"`: `who` the search's name, `initials` from it, `channel` `"Leads"` (the union gains it), `when` `waited {formatWaited(now - waiting_since)}`, `sentence` the question, `quoted: false`, `status: ["Waiting for your answer", "nothing is spent while it waits"]`, `tone: "escalated"`, `warn` when it has waited over twelve hours (the sweep stops it after a day), `cta: { label: "Answer", to: leadQuestionRoute(id) }`, `also: null`, `waitedMs` its wait, so it sorts with the people by how long it has waited. `parts` gains "1 search waiting for your answer" / "N searches waiting for your answer"; `queue.waiting` counts it. `conversationId` and `callId` are null. Today's page draws it with `AttnRow` as every row (`testid` `queue-search`), no glyph.
- **G7. The bridge reads the searches for Today.** `todayOverride.apply` fetches `GET lead-searches` beside the other four, as calls are fetched: a failed read is null and the brief stands. `refreshOnTables` gains `lead_rounds` (a round that stops to ask is an UPDATE on it); `refreshWhen` lets every `lead_rounds` change through (rare, and each one changes what the queue says).
- **G8. Ask says it too.** `AskWaiting` gains `searches: Array<{ id; name; question; to }>`; `waitingFrom` fills it from the queue's `search` rows (and keeps them out of `people`; `more` subtracts only people). `rightNowLine` gains a third clause: "your search for {name} is waiting for your answer" (one) / "{n} searches are waiting for your answer" (es: "tu búsqueda de {name} espera tu respuesta" / "{n} búsquedas esperan tu respuesta"), joined with `;` as the runs are. `rightNowCounts` gains `searches`; the Right now row's pills gain `t.searchWaiting(n)` ("1 search waiting" / "N searches waiting"; es "1 búsqueda en espera" / "N búsquedas en espera"). `needsAnswer` lists each waiting search as an item (`title` the name, `meta` "Leads", `line` the question, `source` a page door to its route) after the people and before the runs. `buildAskSuggestions` is unchanged.
- **G9. Words, once.** The receipt, the refusal words and the summary prefix live in the server and bridge files that say them; the pills and the Right now clauses in `ask-text.jsx` and `ask-answer.ts` as their siblings do; the queue row's words in `today.ts`. `LEAD_QUESTION_WAIT_LINE` is not re-said: the row's second status is its short form.
- **G10. Tests execute, never scan.** The server (`lead-ask.test.mjs`: `amends` on a waiting search sets version and count and carries `amends`, refuses a search not waiting or not the workspace's, `list_lead_searches` says the question; the commit door with `amends` posts the answer and opens on the question's route; `lead-routes.test.mjs`: the route sets the version and count on `include`), contracts (`ask-answer.test.mjs`: `cardOpenRoute` with `amends`, `rightNowLine`/`rightNowCounts`/`needsAnswer` with searches; `today-queue.test.mjs`: the search row, its place in the order, the part and the count; `toTodayVM` without the argument unchanged), the palette (`ask-palette.test.mjs`: `waitingFrom` with a search row, the pill), the finder (`leads-finder.test.mjs`: the prefix names the id, an answer's card commits through Ask's door, a card without `amends` is refused), Today's page (`today-page.test.mjs`: the search row drawn with Answer). No `readCode` assertion is added; the source-scan baseline may only fall. The e2e (added after the merge, branch `test/a-waiting-search-e2e`): the nightly seed holds a second search whose probe waits on its answer, and `today.spec`, `ask.spec` and `leads.spec` read the row, the pill, Brief me and the strip without answering anything (`e2e/waiting-search.ts` refuses and counts every answer and turn).

## 3. Screens

- **Today, a search waiting:** in Your queue, between the people by wait: the search's name, "Leads, waited 3h", the question in full, "Waiting for your answer · nothing is spent while it waits", one button, Answer, which lands on the search's left-out pile with the strip in the accent. The head's line reads "2 need you · 1 search waiting for your answer".
- **Ask, anywhere:** the Right now row: the pills "2 need you" "1 search waiting", the sentence "Ana and Sam are waiting on you; your search for personal trainers in Austin is waiting for your answer." Brief me lists the search after the people with its question and a door. Typed "yes, include the gyms too": the card is the same plan with the answer applied, its summary starting "Answers “Should I include…”"; Approve answers the search and lands on it.
- **Leads, the answer row:** as today: the question, one input, Send my answer; the card that comes back is approved through the same door; the strip goes back to running.

## 4. What must not change

The turn protocol; the plan card's pickers and sentence; the commit door's refusals in the route's words (402, 409, 429); `lead_round_answer` and its `version + 1` rule; the strip's nine endings and six next steps; the question loop and its sweep; Keep and Stop as strip presses; the rail; the phone's takeover and the Stage; the seed's `about`, `thread`, `prompt`, `mode`; the sample Today.

## 5. Not in this phase

A thread id on a search (the answer row's turn still opens a thread of its own); Keep and Stop from Ask (a second proposal kind); the three following a search's run.

## 6. As built (24 Sep 2026, one PR)

Built as the plan says, with these differences found in review:

- **G2:** under `amends` the search row is read before the planner runs, and the plan is built at the stored plan's count, so the card's title and the plan say the same number; `answerPlanOf` still sets version and count. A database error while reading the search is not swallowed into "not in this workspace": only an `AppError` is (the outer catch logs the rest). `waitingQuestionOf` in `server/leads.ts` is the one reader of a waiting round's question (the list tool and the proposal).
- **G3:** the prompt and the tool's note name the strip's real buttons, "Yes, keep going" and "Stop here".
- **G5:** the panel refuses a card whose `amends` names a different search, not only a card without one.
- **G6:** the warn threshold is a module constant; a missing `waiting_since` reads as a wait of zero rather than dropping the row.
- **G8:** three pills wrap (`.ask-now-pills`), centred under the sentence on a phone: the Spanish words outgrow 375px.
- **Not built:** unchanged from section 5.
