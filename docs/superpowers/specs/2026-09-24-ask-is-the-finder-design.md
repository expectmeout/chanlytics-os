# Ask is the finder: design spec

**Date:** 24 Sep 2026 · **Owner:** Ruben · **Status:** approved by Ruben, 24 Sep ("approved as you suggest continue"), phase 1 only · **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-24-ask-is-the-finder.md` (one plan, one PR)
**Base:** written against `origin/main` at `966edb3b` (#986). The Stage (`2026-09-23-ask-the-stage-design.md`, S12 revised by #961) and the Line's protocol stand underneath: the turn, the proposal card, the commit door and the lead finder engine (`docs/decisions/`, release B) are unchanged. Where this spec and the code disagree, the code wins.

## 1. Goal

Ruben, 24 Sep, on the Leads page: the AI lead-generation door is a sparkle "sun" while the app's AI is the three everywhere else; on a phone it opens "a different like chat" (a bottom sheet with a coral mic orb, "Describe the businesses you want", Plan it) instead of the takeover. "For parity we should have the three icon there instead and when it opens I would see that instead of this screen on mobile." Then, after the end-to-end analysis: "approved as you suggest continue."

The goal, in one line: **finding businesses is one Ask turn, so it wears Ask's face, opens Ask's screen, and its plan card is Ask's card; the page keeps only what belongs to a search already running.**

What the analysis found underneath (recorded so the plan's tasks make sense):

- The finder already runs one Ask turn (`leadFinderPlan`), with a hand-written prefix ("On People › Leads, find businesses I could reach: …"), no thread and a surface named `clients`. The server creates a thread for every such turn and titles it from that sentence, so Ask's Earlier fills with "On People › Leads, find businesses I could…"; the finder ignores the thread, so it cannot be resumed. `clients` is not a surface the route accepts, so it is dropped in silence and the prompt never says which page the owner is on.
- The route hand-lists its surfaces and scope kinds instead of reading contracts; that is how the drift went unnoticed.
- Ask already draws the `lead_search` card with the finder's own plan card and commits it through the same door (`via: "ask"`). Two gaps: Open lands on All leads, not the new search, though the commit returns the id; and "Change what I asked" on Ask's card calls the reply editor, which navigates to an inbox thread that does not exist.
- Suggestions are read on a workspace's first day only.
- A running search's questions and next steps (the strip) are the page's, and stay so in this phase.

## 2. The design, F1–F12

- **F1. The door wears the three, once per screen.** On a phone the ✦ is the door: the Leads bar's find square goes, and the bar is lists and search. On a desktop the "Find leads" button keeps its label, its place in the controls row and the F key, and wears a static mark of the three squares (a new `three` glyph in the icon kit), never a second live canvas: one character, one mood (ask.css already hides the top bar's face while the Line is open). The first day keeps its ways and suggestions; its composer slot becomes one primary "Find leads" button with the same mark, and a suggestion picked opens Ask with those words in the field. The sparkle stays only where it means "written by Claude" (a smart list's title).
- **F2. A Leads surface, said once.** `AskSurface` gains `leads`; contracts export `ASK_SURFACES` and `ASK_SCOPE_KINDS` and the route reads both instead of its own sets. `surfaceFor(page, param)` answers `leads` for `clients` with a param that starts with `leads`; the Line asks it with the route's param. The prompt says, for that surface: they are on the Leads page; words that describe businesses (a kind, a place, a count) are a search to propose, `list_lead_searches` then `propose_lead_search`; ask only when the kind or the place is missing. The client sends the owner's words as typed; the prefix and `leadFinderAsk` go, so a thread's title is the owner's own words.
- **F3. Ask at rest on Leads.** The placeholder is "Describe the businesses you want" (es: "Describe los negocios que quieres"). The Right now row stands as everywhere. The page section is labelled "Find businesses" ("Encontrar negocios"): on the first day its chips are the suggestion words (up to three, each sends); after that, "Find more like: {asked}" for the latest two searches; then the usual questions. Under the chips, one dim line, the consent rule: "You can email and call leads. Texts and offers wait for their yes." (es: "Puedes escribir y llamar a los leads. Los SMS y las ofertas esperan su sí."). The bridge feeds `buildAskSuggestions` from `window.__LEADS` (suggestions, searches), which the leads bridge already publishes.
- **F4. The card on the rail.** The `lead_search` card is drawn as today (LeadPlanCard, Approve, "Change what I asked", Not now). "Change what I asked" puts the turn's own question back in the field and focuses it (the reply editor is for replies). Approve commits as today and, on success, goes to the new search: `cardOpenRoute` answers the search's route when the outcome carries `search_id` (contracts' `leadsRoute`), and the card navigates there, which closes Ask (a phone's takeover replaces its entry, S8). The receipt stays in the thread.
- **F5. The page keeps the plan panel.** `LeadFinder` becomes `LeadPlanPanel`: the plan card for a plan born from the strip (more, nearby, look further, carry on, try again), and, when a search stopped to ask, the answer row (the question, one input, "Send my answer", close) that runs `leadAnswerPlan` (the amend turn, now with surface `leads`). No mic, no suggestions, no lock, no spark, no fresh Find. On a phone the panel is in the existing sheet (`LeadPlanSheet`, a handle, a swipe down); on a desktop it folds out above the table as today.
- **F6. Deleted.** The phone composer CSS (the 76px orb, the centred column), the second talk hook and its two toasts in the People page, the `leads-find` tab draft, the finder's Esc-first branch for the composer, `leadFinderAsk`, the composer branch of `leadFinderPlan`, the lock and `LEAD_FINDER_CONSENT` (the sentence lives in Ask's words), `finder-*` testids.
- **F7. Keys.** F on Leads opens Ask (as ⌘K does, on this page). Esc is Ask's. The page's Esc still closes the peek first, then the plan panel, then the Columns menu, then the selection, in that order.
- **F8. Gates and spend.** Unchanged: `PROPOSAL_GATE.lead_search` is `find`, the route's spend and rate policy hold, every new search is `via: 'ask'` (the `people` value remains for the plan panel's strip-born rounds, which do not pass this door).
- **F9. Words, once.** The new words live in `ask-text.jsx` (en, es): `describeLeads`, `findBusinesses` (the section label), `leadsConsent`, `moreLike`. The first day's foot keeps its own sentence (`LEAD_WORDS.firstDay.foot`, contracts).
- **F10. Tests execute, never scan.** Contracts (the surface list, `surfaceFor`, the leads suggestions, `cardOpenRoute` with an outcome), the prompt (`turnSystem` with the leads surface), the route (a `leads` surface accepted), the palette (the Leads surface at rest, the words sent as typed with surface `leads`, Change puts the words back, Approve goes to the search), the finder file (the answer row, the card; the composer tests go), the page (no bar square; the button; the first day's button and pick; F opens Ask; Esc order), the bridge (unchanged), and `e2e/leads.spec.ts` rewritten for Ask (the ✦ on a phone, the button on a desktop). No `readCode` assertion is added; the source-scan baseline may only fall.
- **F11. Versions.** Every legacy file changed bumps its `?v=` in `Chanlytics.html`; contracts' dist is rebuilt and committed.
- **F12. Not in this phase.** Server-side amends (a proposal that says which search it amends, the commit door posting an answer), a waiting search in Today's queue and the takeover's pills, a thread id on a search, the three following a search's run. The strip narrates a run; the three move only while Ask works.

## 3. Screens

- **Phone, Leads at rest:** the bar (All leads 96, search), the rows, the tabs, the ✦. Tap the ✦: the takeover, the three at 52px, "Nobody is waiting on you right now.", "Find businesses" with three chips, the consent line under them, the field at the foot saying "Describe the businesses you want", the one button (mic). Type or hold: the three shrink as the plan streams; the card on the rail; Approve lands on the search, the strip running.
- **Desktop, Leads:** the controls row ends with "⁘ Find leads F"; press it or F: the Stage over the page with the same rows, the field, the band; the card on the rail; Approve closes the Stage and opens the search.
- **First day:** the ways, "Find leads" (the mark), the suggestions; a suggestion opens Ask with its words ready to send.
- **A search that stopped to ask:** the strip in the accent, "Answer" opens the plan panel's answer row (a sheet on a phone), as today, minus the mic.

## 4. What must not change

The turn protocol; the plan card's pickers and sentence; the commit door and its refusals in the route's words (402, 409, 429); the strip's nine endings and six next steps; the question loop; the rail; the map; the peek; the keys J, K, Enter, x, C; the seed's `about`, `thread`, `prompt`, `mode`.
