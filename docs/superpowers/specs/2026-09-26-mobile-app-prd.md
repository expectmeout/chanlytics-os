# The phone app: product requirements and the build plan

**Date:** 26 Sep 2026 · **Owner:** Ruben · **Status:** proposed, for Ruben's "go" · **Checked against:** `origin/main` at
`82c9ed6a` (migration head 00182). Supersedes the roadmap sketched at the end of PR 0
(`2026-09-24-mobile-groundwork-design.md`, which stays the record of what PR 0 built).

Every toolchain fact below was read from the Expo documentation on 26 Sep 2026 (SDK 58 reference,
`docs.expo.dev/versions/v58.0.0`) or from Apple's App Review Guidelines the same day. Where a fact could not be
read from a source it is marked _verify_.

## 1. Why

Chanlytics is used from a phone more than from a desk: the person who answers a handoff, hears a call happened, or
checks the next opening is standing somewhere. The web already runs on a phone (the phone tab bar, the installable
manifest), but a phone cannot be _told_ without an app, a link in a text cannot open one, and a business that looks
for Chanlytics in the App Store finds nothing. Ruben, 26 Sep: "my goal is to build this app so that people can access
chanlytics via the iphone app store so we must build it", and "i rather you go above and beyond".

The app is the same product, on the phone's own terms: native tabs, native sign-in, push, a widget, then surfaces that
stop being web pages one at a time. Nothing a customer of a business sees changes; this is for the business's team.

## 2. What exists (PR 0, merged 24 Sep as #959)

- The document knows it is inside the app from one User-Agent token, `ChanlyticsApp/<version> (ios|android)`, stamped
  before paint (`html.in-app`, `window.__IN_APP`). In-app the web tab bar is not drawn, the billing page shows its
  status and one sentence in place of the Stripe doors (App Store 3.1.3(f)), and the page pads by `--app-inset-bottom`.
- `/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` and `assetlinks.json`, 404 until `APP_LINKS_APPLE` / `APP_LINKS_ANDROID`
  are set.
- `POST /auth/native`: an Apple or Google identity token becomes the cookie session; 303 to this site only.
- Migration 00162: `push_devices`, two definer doors, `jobs.kind` gains `push_send`; `PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/me/devices`.
- `notify` schedules one `push_send` per kept row of a `PUSH_KINDS` kind; the edge sends through Expo's push service;
  a device the _ticket_ reports gone is forgotten.

PR 1 (#1122, merged 26 Sep) put the shell in the tree: `apps/mobile` on SDK 57, five native tabs over the live site,
the bridge in contracts, the first cloud builds. PR 2 (#1128, merged 26 Sep) is push: the receipts job (00189), the badge, the page registering the phone, taps into
their tab. PR 3 (#1130) is native sign-in and the lock. PR 4 is Today on the home screen. PR 5 is account deletion and the version gate. PR 6 is the store pack (`docs/store/`). PR 7 is the call in progress on
the Lock Screen: a push-to-start token on the device's row (00191), the edge speaking to Apple's service directly
(`lib/ops/apns.ts`), one activity per phone as a call starts, ended by the app when the Live page says none is on. PR 8
was the first native screen, the Inbox thread, and the bearer token beside the cookie it needed (decision 0011). It was
retired on 27 Sep (#1176): the web Inbox opens a thread by state, so the native one never opened from the list, and a
second screen for the one thread is a second thing to keep true. The Inbox is the page's own; on a phone a thread is an
entry of its own in the page's history, so Back closes it on the web and in the app. The site's bearer door stays, with
no client yet (decision 0011, amended).
PR 9 was Today, native (#1154), withdrawn on 27 Sep in the review that followed the app's first run: it drew fewer
sentences than the page and in a second theme, over a page it did not replace. The Today tab is the page's own Today.
The same review put the app's theme where the page says it is (the page posts its resolved theme; the shell's chrome
and screens follow it) and reserved the strip under the status bar on both platforms. The same review's other PRs: the account-deletion door fixed for anyone with history (#1162, 00208), the native sign-in door refusing another site's form (#1163), the push guards (#1164), and on the app: a sign-out that signs out (#1173), navigation that holds (#1176), and screens that hold (#1186).

Two facts about the web that shape the app: every page is a hash route under the workspace,
`/w/<slug>#/today|inbox|calendar|clients|...`, and the page already raises the events a shell needs
(`inbox:counts`, `me:changed`, `workspace:changed`). The phone tab bar has four tabs and a Menu button that opens the
navigation drawer.

## 3. Goals and non-goals

**Goals.** An app in the App Store and on Google Play that a team member installs, signs into, is told by, and lives in
every day. Reviewable under Apple 4.2 without argument. Built so that the second native surface costs less than the
first.

**Non-goals for the first release.** A native rewrite of any page; offline editing; the customer-facing side (the
"say yes" page, the workspace's public page) inside the app; iPad and Android tablet layouts beyond what the web
already does; Expo Go (nothing here runs in it: push, universal links, Face ID and the widget all need a development
build).

## 8. Risks

- **The web shell's boot inside five WebViews.** Measured in PR 1 on a mid-range Android phone and an iPhone from 2022. If a tab takes longer than the web does in Safari, the remedy is on the web side (what the shell compiles in
  the browser), not a different app architecture.
- **A 4.2 rejection anyway.** The reply is the list in section 6 and a video of the widget and a push. If it stands,
  PR 7's Live Activity is pulled forward.
- **Cookie longevity in WKWebView.** The session cookie is refreshed by the proxy on every request; a long-idle app
  may come back signed out. The shell treats `{t:"session",state:"out"}` as a return to the sign-in screen, never as
  an error.
- **The tab bar constant (M4).** Proven on iOS 26 and 18 in PR 1; if Expo ships the layout function, the constant goes.
