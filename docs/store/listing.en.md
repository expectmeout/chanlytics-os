## Description

Chanlytics is the receptionist for a business that turns a conversation into a booked appointment. It answers your
customers on WhatsApp, text, the phone and email from your own facts, books the appointment, and hands you the
moments it should not decide.

The app is your team's side of it.

- **Today.** Who needs you, what came in, what the receptionist did, and the day's numbers.
- **Inbox.** Every conversation, on every channel, with the receptionist's answers and yours.
- **Calendar.** What is booked, what is held, and the next opening.
- **People.** Everyone who has ever written or called, and what they asked for.
- **Told.** A handoff, a call, a note for you: on your phone the moment it happens, with the bell's number on the icon.
- **Today, at a glance.** The widget on your home screen and Lock Screen.
- **Yours alone.** Sign in with Apple or Google, and a lock with Face ID.

Chanlytics is for teams whose business runs on the web at chanlytics.com. Billing is managed there.
