## 1. The shell repaints only what changed (defect 17)

Today `setStatus` (`surface-status.ts:39`) calls every listener on every call, `LegacyApp` bumps a tick on each and
re-renders `ChanlyticsApp` with three new inline arrows (`onTheme`, `onLook`, `onSidebar`), and the route gate bumps
its own tick on each (`app-shell.jsx:247`). About seven whole-shell renders per inbound message.

The notice is load-bearing (the frontend plan of 27 September, §1): a surface writes its globals, dispatches its
`surface:*` event and then reports its status, and the whole-shell render is what makes a component that reads a
global without hearing its event draw the new rows. So the change is in three parts, in one step:

- **Status is status.** `setStatus` notifies only when a surface's status, readiness or failure changed.
- **Data is a signal of its own.** `activateOverride` bumps the surface's version after each apply
  (`surface-signal.ts`, new): `versionOf(key)`, `onSurfaceData(keys, fn)`. The shell re-renders when a surface the
  route on screen draws lands (its own, `ROUTE_SURFACE`, and `ROUTE_DRAWS`), not when a stranger does. Where a page
  reads a surface it does not hear, the route names it in `ROUTE_DRAWS` (the audit, below), so no page loses a
  repaint it had.
- **Stable props and a memoized tree.** `LegacyApp` hands `ChanlyticsApp` callbacks made once and memoizes the
  element on the props that can change, so its own re-renders (the workspace list, a preference) reach the shell
  only when a prop did. The route gate bumps only when the route's readiness changed.

Proved by a render-count test on the stub React (the shell's renders per surface refresh, before and after) and a
style-equality recording of the pages, which must move nothing.

## 2. Message events reach the three surfaces that draw messages

Seven surfaces re-read on a message insert or a conversation update: Inbox, Conversations, Today, People, Reports,
Broadcasts and Playbooks. After: Inbox, Conversations and Today. People, Reports, Broadcasts and Playbooks re-read on
their own tables and, for a message, only while their page is on screen; a surface that let an event pass reads
once when its page comes on screen (`surface-freshness.ts`, new). A test runs a message insert through the real
overrides' rules and finds exactly the three off screen.

## 3. The refresh map is exhaustive

Every write in `routes.generated.ts` is in `AFTER` or in `REFRESHES_NOTHING` with the reason, held by a test that
fails a new write in neither (98 of 141 today). Contact edits also re-read Inbox, Conversations and Today, which
carry the person's name and state. The partner verbs go through the map, not a second hand-written refresh.

## 4. The newest read wins; a sign-out stays signed out (defect 52)

- One read at a time per surface: a refresh asked for while one is out waits for it and then reads once more for
  everyone who asked meanwhile, so an older answer can never land after a newer one (`activateOverride`, the path the
  boot, the feed, a write's refresh and Retry all take).
- `signOut` seals the tab's cache before anything else: a snapshot after the seal writes nothing, so a refresh landing
  between the clear and the form's post cannot put the rows back (the probe in `review3/G/`, turned into a test).
- The cache keeps its parsed copy: a snapshot no longer re-reads and re-parses up to 2 MB of the tab's storage.

## 5. Boot carries only what a real workspace draws

- The demo's seeds move under `demo/data/` and into `manifest-demo.ts` where no real file needs them at boot; the real

## 6. State ownership

- `useSurface(key)` over `useSyncExternalStore`, published by the bridge (`window.useSurface`): a component reads
  its surface's version and redraws when it lands. Today, the Inbox and (after #1334) People move to it; the other
  force-update reducers and hand-written `surface:*` listeners stay, listed.
- The role every gate reads comes from the bridge (`legacy-bridge/roles.ts`, `__ME.role` and View-as), and
  `ST_STORE.myRole()` delegates to it (after #1334, which holds `settings.jsx`).
- A paragraph of state rules in `docs/architecture.md`: where server state, client state and a tab's own state live.

## 7. Accessibility

- A `<main>` landmark and a skip link in the shell; focus to the page's heading on a route change.
- One focus-trap helper (`window.trapFocus`, data-bits): focus in, Tab kept inside, Esc, focus restored. Used by every
  `aria-modal` overlay: PhoneSheet (after #1334), Ask's Line, the shortcuts sheet, the notifications panel,
  `st-modal`, the Calendar's panel.
- Names on the icon-only buttons, and the clickable non-controls given a role and keys or made buttons. The a11y
  lint's baseline falls and is banked (`corepack pnpm lint -- --update`).

## 8. Performance

- People and Leads draw a window of rows above about 300 (inside `PeopleTable`, the cursor kept): after #1334.
- People stops re-reading on every message (§2), and its pages load on demand.
- Import leads reads a file under a size cap, and says when it cannot read one (G:1).
