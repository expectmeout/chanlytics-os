## 1. In one page

**What an owner gets, at the end**

- They press Connect once, sign in at Meta, and come back to a page that shows their own ads, what each cost, and who
  each one brought.
- They can pause any ad from the page.
- They make an ad on one screen that AI has already written. They change the amount, the distance or the end date by
  tapping it, and anything else by telling AI.
- Anything Meta would refuse is said before Turn on, with the fix offered.
- Turn on names the most the ad can cost. Nothing runs before that press, and Meta charges their card, never Chanlytics.
- A person who taps the ad writes on WhatsApp, AI answers knowing which ad they tapped, and the page counts who booked.

**The steps**

| Step | What ships | What an owner sees | Waits for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free checks | Meta's own guide read; then dry runs and paused ads on your own account, as each step is built | Nothing | Your sign-in at Meta. No money |
| 1. Keep what an ad click says | One migration, the ad's own words kept on each tap, the owner-only permission to run ads, the shared rules | The page names the ad a person came from, where today it says only "An ad" | Nothing. It can start today |
| 2. Connect and read | Connect, the card, the owner's ads read and counted, Pause and Resume, Disconnect, Reports › Ads, the Connections card | The coral button says Connect. After it: their real ads | Step 1. It works on your own ad account before any review |
| 3. Meta's gates | Clips, descriptions and submissions | Nothing new | Each gate has its own needs (section 7). Meta's clock |
| 4. Make an ad | New ad on one screen, the checks before Turn on, Turn on, Meta's check, a refusal in Meta's words | The coral button says + New | Step 2. Meta's gates, before any business but yours |

Step 3 is a clock, not a build. Its gates open only other businesses. So the build runs 1, 2, 4, with each gate sent as
soon as what it needs exists.

**Where it stands today**

- Real: a person who taps an ad and writes is stamped "An ad", with the ad's bare id. The Ads page reads those counts.
- A mockup on sample data: everything else on the page, in your workspace alone.
- Not built: no ad account, ad or spend is kept; nothing calls Meta's ads; no permission to run ads exists.

**What this plan needs from you** is in section 12.

## 4. The owner's journey, screen by screen

Each screen says what it shows, what it reads, what a press does, and what the mockup's part becomes. Names in
`code font` are the ones 0049 and 0050 already use. Words in quotes are the canvas's.

### 4.1 Nothing connected

- **Shows:** "Ads bring new people to AI.", the last 30 days, how many people wrote from an ad, and the card "Facebook
  and Instagram · Not connected" with what connecting does and "Meta charges your card, never Chanlytics." Under it,
  "Google and TikTok come later." The coral button says Connect.
- **Someone who isn't the owner** sees the same page with no button, and "Only the owner can connect an ad account."
- **Reads:** the first-touch counts, which are real today (`first-touch.ts`, the read Reports › People uses). From step 1
  each count names its ad.
- **Mockup:** kept (`hero.tsx`, the nothing-connected page). It gains the card, and the head stops hiding its button
  in this state (`ads-page.tsx`, `HeadActs`). The three platform rows become the one card and the quiet line.

### 4.2 Connect

- **Press:** Connect opens Meta's own window at once. No sheet of ours.
- **Behind it:** an owner-only route makes a one-time state and sends the browser to Meta's sign-in. Meta returns to a
  callback, which checks the state and the owner's permission again, trades the code on the edge, and keeps the token
  as a Vault secret (`ad-creds-<account id>`). The dashboard holds no token and calls no platform (0049, Security).
- **Reuses:** the code of the Meta sign-in Instagram uses (`packages/channels/src/instagram/meta.ts`: Facebook Login
  for Business with a saved configuration, and a token that belongs to the business). 0049's step P0b pulls it out into
  one Meta client and one sign-in state helper.
- **Its own saved sign-in at Meta.** Read on 19 November: the app holds one saved sign-in, WhatsApp's, and its kind
  cannot be changed. Connect needs a second, of the general kind, which asks for Pages, ad accounts and the ads
  permissions. Making it is a change at Meta and waits for your word.
- **If the window is closed:** the card says "Nothing was connected", and nothing changed.
- **Meta also asks** for an address it can call when an owner removes Chanlytics from Meta's side. It ships with
  Connect (G12).

### 4.3 The card

One card, and one list of checks behind it. The card and New ad read the same list (`adChecks`), so they cannot disagree.

| The card says | When | Its one act |
|---|---|---|
| Checking, line by line | Back from Meta's window | None. It goes on by itself |
| Connected, and who Meta charges and the Page the ads run as | Everything is ready. Folded, at the page's foot | Its caret: change the ad account or the Page, or disconnect |
| "3 of 4 ready", and the one thing missing | A check failed | That check's own act: "Add it in Meta", "Link them" |
| "One question" | Two ad accounts could both be right. The likelier is picked, with why | "Use Chanlytics" |
| "Nothing was connected" | The window was closed | None. Connect is still at the top |
| No ad account, not allowed to run ads on it, Meta has it turned off, connected to another workspace | Each stop the canvas draws | One act each |

- **Each check says what it stops:** reading, pausing, making an ad, or turning one on. A card problem at Meta stops
  Turn on and never hides the numbers. It is still one list (G2).
- **Chanlytics picks** the Page that is linked to the number AI answers, and the ad account that Page's ads run from.
- **Changing either** runs the checks again before anything can be turned on.
- **Chanlytics looks again** when the owner comes back from mending something at Meta. No reload, no second sign-in.
- **"Link them" in one press:** Meta's guide says a Page can be linked to a WhatsApp number through its interface as
  well as by hand. It is seen working on your own account in step 2. If Chanlytics may not do it for an owner, the
  button opens Meta.
- **A quiet line says whether the account has its own spending limit at Meta,** with "Set one in Meta". It is a second
  safety net, and Chanlytics never sets it (section 10).
- **Mockup:** the account chooser and the first-ad checks (`hero.tsx`), the checks' popover (`standing.tsx`) and the
  Connections card all become this one card.

### 4.4 The page, once connected

- **Ready, no ad made here yet:** "2 ads are in your Meta account.", then "Charged to your card by Meta, never through
  Chanlytics. Pause an ad and it stops. Meta still bills what it already showed.", AI's one line proposing the first ad,
  the ads, and the folded card.
- **Ads running:** the sentence ("26 of 114 new people from your ads have booked."), what was spent and what each
  booked person cost, AI's one line, who is waiting on you, each ad with when it ends and what is left, and Results,
  which opens Reports › Ads.
- **Reads:** `adsPage`. A person is counted once, for the ad they first came through (0049, call 5). The cost of a
  booked person is a dash under five booked, and says "so far" while its days are still settling (0049, call 7).
- **What Meta holds is what the page says.** Where the owner changed an ad in Meta itself, the page says Meta's amount
  and end date and that they were changed in Meta. It never changes them back.
- **Mockup:** kept: the page, the figures, the ledger, the line for who is waiting. Removed: the Coming and What came
  back tiles and the Results sheet. The sample, its ten examples and the Example pill leave when the page reads real
  ads.
- **Not drawn on the canvas:** this page on a desk (G1).

### 4.5 An ad's drawer

- **An ad made in Meta:** Pause, always. Resume only where Meta holds a total with an end date and the ad has that
  budget to itself (C1), as a question that names what is left: "Turn back on · up to $180 left, ends 30 Nov".
  Otherwise: "Turn it back on in Meta". A pause of that kind has no Undo here.
- **An ad made here:** as the mockup draws it. Its sentence's words are tapped to change the amount, the end date or the
  area; a raise names its most; a change of words or picture goes back to Meta's check.
- **Meta turned this ad down:** Meta's own words, in quotes. "Fix it" opens the draft with AI's change already made, and
  sends it back to Meta's check on the owner's press. Asking Meta to look again opens Meta (C8).
- **Undo, and Meta's pace.** Meta takes one change to the same ad every 30 seconds. An Undo inside that time is kept and
  sent when Meta will take it, and the row says "Turning it back on…" until Meta confirms.
- **Reads:** `adDrawer`, `adPeople`. **Presses:** the routes `pause`, `resume`, `budget`, `end-date`, `end`, each
  owner-only, each carrying the version it was drawn from (0050, Routes).
- **Mockup:** kept (`drawer/`, `model/drawer/`). Its sentences that change are in section 9.

### 4.6 New ad: one screen

- **Opens** from + New, from AI's line, or from "Run as an ad" on a made piece in Content (6.8). The sentence is there
  at once. The words arrive within seconds, under "AI is writing…" (G18).
- **Shows:** one sentence ("Show First appointment to people within 5 miles, so they message you on WhatsApp, up to $70
  until Tue 2 Dec."), why that amount, the ad as it will look with "1 of 3 versions" and "Your Page" on it, "Tell AI
  what to change", who Meta charges, what the press tells Meta, and "Turn on · up to $70". Under it: "Made paused. Meta
  checks it first, usually within a day."
- **The owner can:** tap the amount, the miles or the date and change it in place, with the button following. Tell AI
  anything else. Press Turn on.
- **The ad as Meta draws it.** Once connected, the preview is Meta's own, where a check on your own account shows that works with the
  sign-in Chanlytics holds. Otherwise it is drawn by Chanlytics and says so (section 10).
- **What would trip it** is said before Turn on, one line with its one act. Section 5.1 is the whole list.
- **Where a draft can't be written yet,** + New says the one thing missing and opens it: a service, the business's
  address, a picture that is fine in ads, or WhatsApp connected (G13).
- **Where each part comes from:**

| Part | From |
|---|---|
| What it offers | Services, by the rules in G5 |
| The picture | Content: a made piece about that service that Content says may run as an ad (6.8). A video before a photo |
| How much, until when | The Calendar's open times, times what a booking from an ad has cost. Under 5 booked from ads, or with no calendar link: Meta's least for the account, said as "A starting amount.", with no number of bookings promised. Meta's least is the account's least for a day times every calendar day the run touches, today and its last day both counted (read 19 November; the day counted on the paused-ad check, 20 November) |
| Where | The business's address, 5 miles to begin with, and from 1 to 50. Meta reaches people who live there or were there lately, and offers no other choice |
| The words | The ad writer (6.7) writes three versions, checked by section 5.1 before the owner sees them |
| Who sees it | Adults only, always: a least age of 18, which Meta keeps as a limit with its automatic audience on. Meta requires it for cosmetic services, and it costs the others nothing |
| Where it shows | The feed, stories and Reels, on Facebook, and on Instagram where the Page's Instagram account is known. Named, so Meta adds no place by itself. WhatsApp Status, Messenger, Threads and other apps are left out of the first version |

- **Three versions are three ads at Meta** (read 19 November). Each version is its own ad, under one part that says
  who sees it and one total, in a campaign of its own. The owner sees one ad with "1 of 3 versions". Meta checks each
  version by itself and can turn down one while two run, and a tap names the version it came from. Meta's way to put
  several texts in one ad shows any headline with any text, a pair nobody approved, and its page does not cover an ad
  that looks for conversations.
- **Meta's own changes to the picture and words are off.** Each is its own switch at Meta now, so each is switched
  off by name, and what Meta kept is read back before anything is turned on.
- **One picture is shown in every place, so it is the feed's shape:** 4:5 or square (Meta takes it as wide as
  1.91:1), at least 600 pixels wide, which stories and Reels show whole. A video only from 3 to 60 seconds. A tall
  picture for stories and Reels alone waits for a way to give each place its own. Ads asks Content for the shape and
  never crops (6.8).

- **Mockup:** replaced. The five steps, the three ways for people to reach you, the four budget choices, "Runs on",
  "Let Meta adjust my photo and wording" and the Review step go. The sentence, the preview and the checks' words are
  reused (`new/new-sentence.tsx`, `drawer/preview.tsx`, `model/words.ts`).

### 4.7 Turn on, and after

All of this is 0050's calls 3 to 6, with the approved additions and C7.

1. **The press** names the most it can cost and carries it. A press drawn from an old amount is refused.
2. **The press is also the owner's declaration** to Meta of what kind of ad it is. The answer is kept with what the
   owner approved.
3. **A dry run first.** Each thing is sent to Meta as a check that makes nothing (5.2), just before it is made. The
   campaign's comes before anything exists in the owner's account. The checks of what sits under it need it to exist,
   so a refusal there leaves a paused campaign with nothing in it, which is removed (read 19 November).
4. **Setting up:** everything is made paused, read back from Meta, and compared with what was approved: the amount, the
   end, the paused state, and the declaration. A difference stops it.
5. **Then** it is switched on from the bottom, so the last switch is the one that lets it start. The page says "In
   review" while Meta checks it, and "On" only once Meta does.
6. **Nothing you did not approve is ever switched on.** A launch that stops part way pauses or removes what it made.
   What cannot be removed stays named and paused, and the page says "Couldn't finish".
7. **Where Meta's answer never arrived,** nothing is sent again. The ad is found by its tagged name and followed until
   Meta says what it is. If that takes longer than a few minutes the page says "Couldn't confirm it's on", with
   "Check", and the bell rings.
8. **A launch in flight stops** if the owner cancels, disconnects, or the account is changed.

### 4.8 Signed out

- **Shows:** at the top, in amber: Meta signed Chanlytics out, which ad was on at that moment, and that Chanlytics can't
  read or pause it until the owner connects again (C6). Every number keeps its last date ("Updated Sat 14 Nov"). The
  coral button says Connect again.
- **Behind it:** a read that Meta refuses for a withdrawn sign-in marks the account, keeps the last good numbers, and
  rings the bell.

### 4.9 Disconnect

From the card's caret. Never refused (0050, call 8, as amended).

1. **With an ad running:** "Pause 1 ad and disconnect", or "Keep it connected", and a quieter door, "Disconnect
   without pausing".
2. **Disconnecting anyway**, or where the pause can't be confirmed because Meta isn't answering: it first says the ad
   keeps running, Meta keeps charging, and after this it can only be stopped in Meta. Then "Disconnect anyway".
3. **After:** the ad stays listed as "Left in Meta", with whether it was on when access ended, and "Open Meta". The
   coral button says Connect.

What happens to Chanlytics' access (C2): it stops being used at once. Meta is asked to withdraw it. The page says it is
withdrawn at Meta only when Meta confirms. Until then it says where the owner can remove Chanlytics in Meta themselves,
and Chanlytics asks again with its own key.

### 4.10 Reports, Settings and the bell

- **Reports › Ads** is the one place totals are drawn. It reads `adsReport`. The mockup's lens is kept
  (`reports/lenses/ads.tsx`); it already reads the Results sheet's fold, which moves with it.
- **Settings › Connections** shows the same card as the page.
- **The bell:** for the connection, a sign-out and a payment problem. For ads, 0050's call 15 stands: an ad was not
  approved, a card could not be charged, an ad ended or spent its total, nobody has come from an ad in seven days (G9).

## 5. Nothing is sent that Meta is likely to refuse

Three things can be refused: an ad, a request, and Chanlytics itself. The house rule is to check first and say what to
change, so nothing comes back declined. Each check below runs before the owner presses, and offers its fix as one act.

### 5.1 An ad, before Turn on

Meta checks every ad. These are the refusals a small business's ad meets most, from Meta's own rules.

| What is checked | Why Meta refuses it | What the owner is offered |
|---|---|---|
| Words that speak about the reader: "Are you…", "your wrinkles" | An ad may describe the service, never the person reading it. Meta's own example: "Do you have diabetes?" is refused, "New diabetes treatment available" is allowed | AI's rewrite, shown, as one press |
| Words that make someone feel bad about how they look, or promise a sure result | Meta's rules on health and appearance | AI's rewrite |
| A before-and-after picture | Not allowed to show an ideal result, and restricted for cosmetic services | The next picture that is fine in ads |
| A picture or video in the wrong shape or length for where it shows | Meta refuses or crops it | The same piece in a shape Meta takes, or the next one |
| A video with licensed music, or a person who has not said yes to ads | Rights | Never offered in a draft at all. Content answers this, not Ads (6.8) |
| A price that disagrees with Services, or any price while AI is set to never quote prices | Chanlytics' own check (0050, call 7) | "Take the price out" |
| An ad about a job, housing, financial products, politics or gambling | Meta's special kinds, which lose the area and age choices the draft rests on | Not run here in the first version, with "Open Meta" (G7) |
| An amount under Meta's least for the account: its least for a day times every calendar day the run touches | Meta refuses the request | The nearest amount Meta takes |
| An amount over what Meta lets a new account spend in a day | Meta's own limit, which no page lets Chanlytics read. Meta's dry run is the check (5.2) | Meta's own words, and a smaller amount |
| An end date too close to its start | Meta refuses the request. No page names its shortest run, so the rule here is a whole day, until the paused-ad check shows Meta's own | The nearest date Meta takes |
| The Page, the WhatsApp number, the payment method, the account's standing | The checks on the card | Each check's own act |

- **AI's own words pass these before the owner ever sees them.** A draft that fails is rewritten first.
- **They run again** after every change, the owner's or AI's, and once more at the press.
- **They are rules in code with tests.** The model writes and rewrites words. It never decides whether a check passed.
- **Meta's rules move.** The list is read against Meta's pages before step 4 is built, and each rule carries the date it
  was read.

### 5.2 A request, before it is sent

- **Meta's dry run.** Meta's interface takes a request as a check alone: it runs its rules and makes nothing. Every
  make and every change goes as a dry run first. On Meta's pages for every part of an ad (read 19 November): the
  campaign, the part that says who sees it, the creative and the ad. The ad's can also ask for Meta's own look at the
  words and the picture. To see on your account: that it holds for an ad that opens WhatsApp, and whether a dry run
  counts against the account's score.
- **A create carries a key of ours,** which Meta's page says makes it safe to repeat: sent again with the same key
  inside seven days, Meta answers with what the first made. A switch takes no key. Whether a launch sends a create
  again with it, where decision 0050 says it never does, is yours to decide with the job that makes an ad.
- **Meta's limits are checked before a press, not after it:**

| Meta's limit, read on Meta's page | What the page does |
|---|---|
| One change to the same thing every 30 seconds | A second change waits its turn and says so (4.5) |
| An amount changed at most 4 times an hour | The amount says when it can next be changed, before the owner tries |
| An ad account can carry a daily spending limit at Meta, which also limits how many ads it may make | Chanlytics cannot read it: Meta's page for an ad account lists nothing for it (read 19 November). The dry run is the check, and where Meta still refuses, its own words are shown |
| Until the higher tier is granted, an ad account takes very few calls: a score of 60, a read costing 1 and a write 3, easing over 5 minutes | Reads are spread out, follow Meta's own usage figures, and stop early. A refused call counts against the error rate the higher tier asks for, so none is sent that will be refused |

- **Where Meta refuses anyway,** its own sentence for the owner is shown in quotes, with the one thing to do. Its code
  goes to Health, never to a screen.
- **One version of Meta's interface,** named in one place, with the date Meta retires it on the calendar. Meta's guide
  for these ads uses v27.0 in its examples (read 18 November).

### 5.3 Chanlytics itself, in Meta's review

Read on Meta's own pages. Each line is a reason Meta gives for turning a submission down.

- **Ask only for what a screen uses.** Asking for a permission the app does not need is a common refusal.

| Permission | The screen that shows it used |
|---|---|
| `pages_show_list` | The card: the Pages Chanlytics picks from |
| `pages_read_engagement` | The card: the Page's name, and the ad shown as that Page |
| `ads_read` | The page: the owner's ads and Meta's spend |
| `ads_management` | Pause on an ad; Turn on |
| `pages_manage_ads` | Turn on: an ad made for the Page |

- **One clip for each permission,** never several in one, and a written description beside each clip.
- **The whole sign-in,** from signed out to signed in.
- **At least one real, successful call with each permission** before sending.
- **A way in for the reviewer:** a Chanlytics sign-in, on a workspace where Connect shows. If they cannot get in, the
  whole submission is refused. So D7's list holds the review workspace beside yours.
- **Reviewers use their own Meta test accounts,** which most likely have no WhatsApp number linked to a Page. Each
  permission needs a path that works without one (G14).
- **The higher tier is asked for on its own:** 500 calls in 15 days, with under 15% errors in the last 500. Step 2
  running on your account makes the calls, and 5.2 keeps the errors down.
- **Your own ad account needs none of this.** Meta's page says the ordinary level is enough where an app manages only
  its own account.

### 5.4 When Meta still says no

- **An ad:** Meta's words in quotes, "Fix it" with AI's change made, and a door to Meta to ask for a second look.
- **A request:** nothing was made, because the dry run came first. The owner sees what to change.
- **The review:** mended and sent again. No business but yours depends on it until it passes.

- **On the page, one line.** When it speaks is rules in code with tests, and no model: propose the first ad once an

### 6.4 When Meta does not answer

- **A read that fails** keeps the last good numbers and says "Couldn't update · Updated 2 PM".
- **A write that got no answer** is never sent again. It is settled by reading what exists at Meta (4.7, point 7).
- **A pause that can't be confirmed** during Disconnect leads to 4.9, point 2.
- **Meta's own outage** never stops an owner from taking access away.

### 6.5 Privacy, and what is erased

- What an ad said goes with the person. Meta's days are kept 25 months, and the privacy page says so (0049, call 11).
- No click id is kept until the step that needs it, and until health and cosmetic businesses are decided (C3). The app
  has no way today to tell such a business apart. That decision needs one.
- Nothing about health is sent back to Meta.
- One business's ads never inform another's. No figure, word or picture of one workspace reaches a draft for another.
- A member can never read where a token is kept.

### 6.6 At full volume

Planned for thousands of connected businesses from the first step, not for today's one.

- **Hear first, read second.** Meta sends a notice when an ad's standing changes. Chanlytics subscribes for each
  connected account and reads only what the notice names. The clock is the backstop, as it is for other providers. To
  confirm: that the notice covers Meta's check of an ad.
- **The clock's three paces** (0049): every 15 minutes for an ad being set up, in review or in its first two days;
  hourly for every connected account; once a day for a 14-day look back, which settles the numbers. The clock asks the
  edge only when a connected account exists, and an event is written only when something changed.
- **Each account is its own job,** spread over the hour, with Meta's usage figures read from every answer. An account
  near its limit is left until Meta says it may be read again.
- **Totals are counted in the database, whole** (C4).
- **To time before the loop is relied on,** as the house rule asks: one real hourly read of your account, end to end,
  and how many accounts one pass can hold.

### 6.7 The ad writer: AI's skill for ads

Chanlytics already keeps the instructions for one job apart from what AI always carries, as a skill in its own format
(`packages/contracts/src/ask/skills/skill.ts`). There are two today: the customer message writer and the video maker.
The video maker's guide was moved out of a tool's description on 13 November, when that block stood two characters
under its limit. Ads adds a third skill, built the same way.

It does not use Anthropic's hosted skills. Those load a folder into a code-running container, which suits making
files, not a turn inside the app. The code already says so.

**Its parts**

| Part | Holds | Lives in |
|---|---|---|
| Its words | Who it is, its jobs, its rules, worked examples, what it never does, and a short guide for AI's own turn | `apps/dashboard/src/server/ask/skills/ad/skill.ts` |
| What it is handed | The business (its name, its tone, its word for an appointment); the service; the facts the owner stands behind for an ad; what the picture shows and how it sounds; the area; the language; Meta's lengths for each place the ad shows; and for a refused ad, Meta's own words | `packages/contracts/src/ask/skills/ad/brief.ts` |
| What code holds its answer to | Section 5.1, one code for each thing that can be wrong. A rule is said to the writer in its words and kept here, never on the prompt alone | `apps/dashboard/src/server/ask/skills/ad/checks.ts` |
| How it is judged | A rubric and a test set of made-up businesses in several trades, some of whose ads Meta would refuse | `packages/contracts/src/ask/skills/ad/rubric.ts`, `tooling/evals/skills/ad.json` |
| Its place in the list | One entry in the skills' registry, and a purpose of its own at the gateway, so what ad writing costs is read apart from everything else (G19) | `apps/dashboard/src/server/ask/skills/registry.ts`, the edge's gateway |

**Its three jobs**

1. Write an ad's words for a draft: three versions.
2. Change a draft from "Tell AI what to change". It answers with a change from the closed list in G8, never free text.
3. Write a refused ad again from Meta's own words. This is what "Fix it" opens.

**Its rules, in short**

- Describe the service, never the person reading.
- Say nothing the brief does not hold: no price, discount, date, number, promise or result.
- No words that make someone feel bad about how they look, and no sure results.
- One thing to do: message on WhatsApp.
- Three versions are three angles on the same facts.
- The business's own tone, in its language, English or Spanish.
- Inside Meta's lengths.
- Its examples name no real business and no one trade.
- It never decides the amount, the area, the dates, or whether a check passed. Those are rules in code.

**The hard tests an answer must pass:** Meta would approve it; it holds nothing the brief does not; it never speaks about
the reader. The soft ones are the craft: one call to act, the business's voice, each version a different angle.

**How it reaches a turn**

- By the words asked, in English or Spanish. This exists today.
- By AI reading the skill's guide when it needs it. This exists today.
- By the page: on the Ads page, AI's turn carries the ads guide. New, and small.
- As the skills grow past a handful, each keeps one short line that is always loaded, and those lines are held under a
  measured limit, as the tools already are.

**The ads guide** is the skill's short form for AI's own turn: what AI hands the writer and what it decides itself, when
it proposes an ad, how it answers "why was my ad refused" from Meta's words, and that it never spends, never promises a
result and never says another business's numbers.

**Who calls the writer:** New ad's own route, and AI's turn when the owner asks AI for an ad. Both build what the writer
is handed with one builder, and the test set uses the same one, so the tests judge the question the product asks.

**Where the writer does not answer** (it ran out of time, the day's AI limit is reached, the workspace is paused, or its
words failed the checks twice): New ad says "Couldn't write the words", with "Try again". An ad with no words that passed
cannot be turned on.

**What it costs:** to be computed from the call's own text, not measured, because a lane holds no model key. The real
figure is the ledger's after the first deploy, or one real call on your word.

**Tests:** as the message writer has: each check with words that pass and words that fail, the skill inside a turn, and
the test set. The test set spends money, so it runs only on your word.
