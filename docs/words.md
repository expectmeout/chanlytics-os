# The words on a screen

Every word an owner or a member reads in Chanlytics follows this page: what to call each thing, which words never reach a
screen, how a page says what happened, and the shape of each thing it says. [glossary.md](glossary.md) says what each
thing _is_, storage included; this page says how a screen _says_ it.

A screen's word never changes what a thing is or what state it is in. It names the same fact the contracts hold, in the
owner's words. The words stay where they live today, in the view models and each page's words file, close to what they
describe: this page is the rule they follow, not one list of every string. It holds wherever a word is written: a page,
a view model, a refusal or an alert the server writes, an email, and what AI says back.

Decided by Ruben on 14 November 2026, after a count of every string the dashboard shows (the last section), and revised
twice the same day: after a review, and after a last walk of the app at a phone's width.

## The voice

Chanlytics writes like a brand, not like a system: calm, exact and short. The product does the work, and the words get
out of the way. These are Apple's rules for interface writing (Human Interface Guidelines, Writing), taken as the house's.

1. **Most important first, then stop.** Check each word earns its place. Read it aloud: if it sounds like a log, rewrite it.
2. **Say it once.** A page does not repeat its own name, a badge does not repeat the line under it, and an age is not
   said twice ("6d" beside "Waiting 6d").
3. **One name for each thing** (the list below), and the same name on every page.
4. **No "we", no "oops", no apology.** "Couldn't load", not "We couldn't load this". Where someone must act for the owner,
   the actor is Chanlytics: "Chanlytics files it for you".
5. **Possessives sparingly.** "Goal", not "Your goal"; "Customers", not "Your customers". "Your" stays only where it tells
   the owner's thing from someone else's ("your number" beside "their number").
6. **Buttons are verbs.** "Connect", "Send", "Turn on", "Delete list". A flow says Get started, then Continue, then Done.
7. **Say only what is known.** Done is said once it is done. An estimate says it is one ("about 400 replies"). What AI
   suggests is not said as what AI did.
8. **A problem sits beside what it is about,** blames nobody, and says the one thing to do ("What happened, said exactly").
9. **Offer the way through, not the rule.** Where a provider's rule stands in the way, AI does the work and the page
   offers the act: "Rafael hasn't written in 6 days · Send follow-up", never the rule explained and left with the owner.
10. **Tone fits the moment.** Plain and direct for a problem. A little lift for good news: a booking is good news.
11. **A setting is a sentence** with its value in it: "AI replies after 30 seconds unless you stop it · Change". Never a
    grid of kinds and modes, and a level is a word ("Only when certain"), never a percentage. To send someone to a
    setting, give a button that opens it, never its path ("Update it in Settings › Billing").
12. **Tap on a phone.** Never "click".
13. **No backend words, no provider words, no codes** (the lists below).

## AI

The product's AI is called **AI**. Chanlytics is the AI.

- **No article**, the way a product name has none: "AI answered 12 calls", "AI is paused". Not "the AI" or "your AI".
- **Never** Ask, receptionist, agent, assistant, bot, Claude or model on a screen.
- **One tag for anything AI wrote or did:** the AI's mark (the three squares, ink at rest, coral only while it works) and
  the word AI. "Written by AI" where a sentence is needed.
- **The search bar says "Ask Chanlytics".** Ask is the verb there; Chanlytics is the AI.
- **A name the owner gives it is for customers.** Settings lets an owner name what answers their customers; customers
  hear that name, and the owner's screens still say AI.
- **AI's own answers say the same.** Its instructions and its tools' words use this page's words, since what AI says
  back is the most-read text in the product.
- **In its answers AI says I,** for what it does and what it answered customers ("I answered 12 of 16 messages"). A
  reply that already went says when it went ("I replied at 12:57 AM"): "I sent" with no time reads as something done
  in that chat, which only a press on a card does.
- **Not on a screen, so not bound:** the greeting callers hear says "AI assistant" where the business wrote no line
  of its own (decision 0051, amended 18 November 2026; the owner's own words to callers are the owner's); chanlytics.com may still describe the job ("an AI receptionist"); the code keeps `ask`
  and `receptionist` as names.

| Written by your receptionist                            | Written by AI                           |
| Made by Ask                                             | (mark) AI                               |

## Words that never reach a screen

**The code's words.** tenant, row, column, record (as storage), database, server, API, token, model, prompt, surface,
pane, OS, render, drawn, read (as "loaded"), sync, queue, job, run, batch, payload, webhook, config, configuration,
provision, credentials, heartbeat, veto, policy, matrix, action class, slug, ID, null, undefined, instance, env.

**The providers' words.** template, WhatsApp version, 24-hour window, utility, marketing (as a category), tier, quality
rating, carriers, registration, error and its number. Provider names the owner never met: Twilio, Retell, Resend, Apify,
Supabase, Vercel, Anthropic, Claude, ElevenLabs, MiniMax. Meta where the owner knows WhatsApp. A provider's status words
(paused, refused, rejected, disabled, declined, approved, pending, in review) are said as what they mean for the owner,
never as the provider's word.

**Where a video is kept.** shelf, copy (of a file), cache, checksum, storage, and the video platform's name. A video
made in Chanlytics or added from a phone is said as any video: it plays, or "Getting your video ready…", and no sentence
of it names a place (`copy-words.ts`, held by `tests/files/screens/a-made-video-says-no-storage-word.test.mjs`). Only a
video the owner keeps in their own Google Drive is said to be there, since that place is theirs.

**Allowed:** what the owner typed or chose. The name of a service where the owner connects it, signs in to it, pays it
or fixes something in it: Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Gmail, Outlook, Meta and Meta Ads Manager on Ads,
Stripe on a receipt. The AI limit's own number in tokens ("250k") until AI has answered enough to say it in replies
(contracts' `budgetDial` already decides which). The Console's staff pages.

**Hidden is not lost.** What a provider said and its code stay in Health, where support reads them and finds the
incident; a screen says what it means.

## What happened, said exactly

A page says the state a thing is really in. Asked, taken, sent, delivered, held, failed and unknown stay different
states, and each has its own words. The client already tells several of them apart (contracts' `refusalOf`: a read that
failed, a write that may have gone through, a role that may not, a wait the server asked for); a page says what it hands
over, and adds nothing it does not know.

| What happened                           | Says                                                  | Its act                           |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| A read failed                           | Couldn't load the calendar                            | Try again                         |
| Done                                    | The result: "Sent to 12 people"                       | Undo, where it can be undone      |
| Taken, still under way                  | "Sending…", "Scheduled for Thu 10 AM"                 | None                              |
| A write got no answer: it may have gone | Couldn't confirm it went through                      | Check. Never Try again before     |
| Partly done                             | "Saved 12 of 15 · 3 couldn't be saved"                | See the 3                         |
| It tries again by itself                | "Waiting to send · tries again at 2:40 PM"            | None                              |
| Left, then not delivered                | Didn't reach them, and the reason                     | The way that can still reach them |
| Can't be done as it is                  | Why, and what would change it                         | That change                       |
| The member's role may not               | "Only an owner can do this"                           | None                              |
| Out of date                             | "Updated 2:14 PM", and "Couldn't update" if it failed | Try again                         |
| Something Chanlytics can't know         | A quiet line: who doesn't say, and what settles it    | None. Never a check left open     |

### Where a post stands

A made piece says where it stands once, and every screen says the same: its tile, its open status, its button, its
list of places, Posted, the Week, the bell and what AI is told all read one answer (contracts' `pieceStandingOf`, 24
November 2026), in one table's words (contracts' `STANDING_WORDS`, a column for each screen; no other module writes
these sentences). The unit is a place and a kind: an Instagram post and an Instagram story are two.

| Where it stands                            | Its tile                                | Its line in the open piece                  | Its button                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| It never went out                          | Ready to post                           | None                                        | Post                        |
| Set for its time                           | Set for Saturday                        | Goes up Sat, 9:00 AM                        | None: it goes by itself     |
| Going up                                   | Posting to Instagram                    | Posting to Instagram                        | None                        |
| Up                                         | On Instagram · Monday                   | On Instagram · Mon, 9:02 AM                 | Post to Google              |
| Held or refused                            | Didn't post                             | Didn't post · and why                       | Post again                  |
| Sent, and the place never said it holds it | Couldn't confirm it's on Instagram      | Couldn't confirm it's on Instagram          | It's there · It's not there |
| Its story ran its day                      | Story ended · Sunday, in grey           | Story ended · Sun, 9:02 AM                  | Post again                  |
| Taken down from here                       | Taken down · Tuesday, in grey           | Taken down · Tue, 4:10 PM                   | Post again                  |
| Removed on the place itself                | Removed on Instagram · Tuesday, in grey | Removed on Instagram · noticed Tue, 4:10 PM | Post again                  |

"Ready to post" is only for a piece that never went out. A story is up for a day: after it, no screen says it is up,
and its line says when it ended. Posted lists what is up and the stories that ran their day, each of those marked
("Instagram · Saturday · Story ended"), and its count counts the same rows; a post that came down, or whose piece was
deleted, is in neither.

**"Taken down" is the owner's own press.** A post deleted on Instagram or a Facebook Page itself, by the owner there or
by the place, is "Removed on Instagram", with when Chanlytics noticed, and one quiet sentence under its line:
"Chanlytics didn't take it down. It was deleted on Instagram itself." Its bell line says the same: "Removed on
Instagram · The finish" over that sentence, never "Taken down". A Google post that came down says "Taken down"
either way for now: the older Remove from Google press keeps nothing that tells the two apart.

**A post nobody could confirm is asked about, never guessed.** Instagram, or a Page, took the post and was never read
holding it. For ten minutes it is still "Posting to Instagram". After that its tile and its open piece say "Couldn't
confirm it's on Instagram", in amber, Needs you lists it, and its line asks the owner, who can look: "Instagram never
said it has this post. Look at the account, then say which.", with two presses, "It's there" and "It's not there".
"It's there", and it is up from now. "It's not there", or Instagram itself saying it does not hold the post, and it
never went up: "Didn't post · Instagram doesn't have it.", with Post again. It is never "Taken down", since nothing was
up to take down. Where its numbers arrive, they are proof enough that it is there, and nobody is asked.

**AI says what the page says.** Asked whether a post is up, or why one did not go, AI reads where each piece stands
from the same answer its tile is drawn from, a clause for each post: "up on Instagram since Nov 21 · 5:19 PM", "set to
go up on Instagram tomorrow 9:00 AM", "not posted on Instagram: it waits for its owner", "removed on Instagram itself,
not taken down from here". It never guesses a standing, and never says "taken down" of a post removed on the place.

**A removed post's ad is said, never paused by itself.** Take down, here, pauses the ads made from a piece. A post
removed on Instagram itself does not: the owner did not press anything here. So where an ad made from the piece is
still running, its line under Where it goes turns amber and says "Its post was removed on Instagram. The ad is still
running.", with one press, "Pause the ad", and the bell's line ends "Its ad is still running: open the piece to pause
it."

**A piece's numbers say whether more are coming.** While a later read comes: "Reel: couldn't be read this time" and
"Next at 3 days." Once none will (it came down, its story's day ended, or its last read is past), the same facts are
said for good: "Story: its numbers couldn't be read before it ended", "Read before the story ended.", "Its numbers
weren't read while it was up." Never "this time", and never a next read that will not come.

two are one act, and each ending is said once, in one set of words (`packages/contracts/src/content/plan/week-endings.ts`):

### A problem, said right

Three parts, in this order:

1. **What happened**, in the owner's words: "Couldn't send", "Card declined", "WhatsApp is busy".
2. **Whether it mends itself**, and when: "Sends again in a few minutes". A state that mends itself is waited out and said
   as a wait, never as a failure.
3. **The one thing to do**, as a button: "Update card", "Check", "Call them". None when it mends itself.

| Kind            | Says                                            |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Mends itself    | WhatsApp is busy · Sends again in a few minutes |
| The owner acts  | Card declined · Update card                     |
| Chanlytics acts | Texting setup paused · Chanlytics is on it      |
| Can't be done   | Their number isn't on WhatsApp · Call them      |

**Never:** an error code, the raw text of an error, a provider the owner never met, "error", "failed" in a sentence,
"something went wrong" with nothing to do, Try again where the first try may have gone, the same failure said twice (a
"Didn't send" badge and a "didn't go" line under it), or a comfort the page has not checked ("Everything else is current,
and AI keeps working" is said only when it is known).

Before a refusal can happen, the product checks for it and says what to change, so nothing comes back declined.

## One number for each idea

- **A count with one name is one count,** the same on every page that shows it. "Needs you" is counted once: Today, the
  menu's badge and the Inbox say the same number.
- **A different count has a different name** ("No reply yet", "Older than a week"), never the same name with another
  rule behind it.
- **A number says its time once, in words:** "Today", "New since you last looked" (a person's History), "Last 30
  days". Never a bare date and time ("Since Wed 2:32 PM").
- **Two facts never share a word.** Appointments on today's calendar and bookings made today are different facts:
  "6 appointments today" in the day and "3 booked" among the figures, never "6 booked" over "3 booked".
- **A count can always be finished.** It counts only what Chanlytics can read. A fact a provider does not share is a
  quiet line that says so and what settles it, never a check that stays open: the Ads card said "3 of 5 ready" forever
  until 20 November 2026, for a Page's WhatsApp number Meta never shares, and says "Ready" now, with "Meta doesn't share
  whether your Page is linked to … Meta checks it when an ad is made." under its checks.
- **A thing is said once on a page** (decision 0137). What waits on you is Needs you's row and the one count; news is
  the bell's. A card or a line that says a row's fact again, with its own count, is the bug: "8 emails wait for your
  approval" over "Your next 5 emails are ready for your approval".

What needs the owner is grouped by the act, and there are three:

## The shape of each thing a page says

| Element           | For                                                    | Words                                                                                                       | Aim                    | Kit piece               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Page title        | The page's name                                        | A noun. Never repeated by the page                                                                          | 1-2 words              | the shell's             |
| Section heading   | A group on a page                                      | A noun. No sentence beside it: a reason goes behind **Why**                                                 | 1-3 words              |                         |
| Button            | An act                                                 | Verb + thing ("Delete list"). One filled button per group                                                   | 1-3 words              | Button                  |
| Badge             | A state                                                | The state alone, from the lists above. Never repeats the line under it                                      | 1-2 words              | StatePill               |
| Line under a name | One or two facts                                       | Facts joined by " · ", no full stop                                                                         | 8 words, 48 characters |                         |
| Numbers           | Counts                                                 | One line: "3 booked · 12 answered", never a row of cards                                                    | 3 facts                |                         |
| Notice            | A fact the page noticed, with its act                  | The fact, its one act, its ×. The reason goes behind **Why**                                                | 12 words + the act     | Notice                  |
| Toast             | What a press just did                                  | The result, then Undo or one act: "Sent to 12 people · Undo"                                                | 6 words                | useToast                |
| Problem           | Something didn't happen                                | "What happened, said exactly" above                                                                         | 12 words + the act     | FailedRead, Notice      |
| Field error       | What to type                                           | What to do, not what is wrong: "Use a full email address"                                                   | 10 words               | FieldError              |
| Confirm           | An act that can't be undone, or that reaches customers | Names the thing: "Delete Regulars?" One line on what happens. Yes is the verb ("Delete list"), No is "Keep" | 1 line + 2 buttons     | Confirm                 |
| Empty             | Nothing here yet                                       | What will be here, then the one act                                                                         | 8 words + the act      | EmptyState              |
| Loading           | A wait past a second                                   | "Loading the calendar…", or a still outline of what comes                                                   | 4 words                |                         |
| Tooltip           | The name of a control that has only an icon            | Its name, which the control also carries for a screen reader. Never the only place a fact lives             | 5 words                | Tooltip, never `title=` |
| Why               | Reasons, rules, sources, how a number is counted       | Opens in place: a popover on a desk, a sheet on a phone                                                     | as needed              |                         |
| Placeholder       | An example of what to type                             | An example, never an instruction: "name@example.com"                                                        | 4 words                |                         |
| Alert (the bell)  | Something happened while the owner was away            | What happened and to what. One alert for one ongoing thing, updated in place, never a new one each day      | 8 words                |                         |
| Email subject     | An alert by email                                      | The same words as its alert                                                                                 | 8 words                |                         |
| Menu              | More acts on one thing                                 | Opens from its control's caret, never from three dots. Each act a verb                                      | 1-3 words an act       | Menu                    |
| Sample            | Demo data                                              | One "Sample" badge, never a suffix in a name. Sample words follow this page: a prospect reads them first    | 1 word                 | StatePill               |

The lengths are aims, not limits: a clear twenty words beat ten that leave out what matters. Past its aim, the reasons
go behind **Why**. Never behind Why: what it costs, what consent it needs, what can't be undone, and who may do it. Those
stay in full where the owner decides. A page never carries a paragraph in its flow otherwise.

## A phone first

Owners open Chanlytics on a phone, between customers, for seconds at a time. Every page is written and judged at a
phone's width before a desk's.

- The first screen shows the page's content: one row of controls above it at most.
- The page's name is said once, never again as a tab or a heading under the title.
- A conversation's foot is for the reply: one line of what stands in the way at most, with its one act.
- Each page answers one question at a glance (Today: what needs me; Calendar: what is booked and what is open).

## Everyone can read it

- Every control has a name a screen reader says, an icon-only one included; taking a `title=` away keeps its name.
- A problem is announced as it appears (FieldError and a failure's toast already are).
- Nothing important lives only in a tooltip or on a hover: a phone shows neither.
- Words cut short to fit can be read whole with a tap.
- When a sheet or a question closes, focus goes back to what opened it.

## Punctuation, case, numbers, time

- **Sentence case** everywhere: buttons, headings, tabs, badges ("The Console" is "Console"; "Agent Fleet" is gone).
  Names and initials keep their capitals: WhatsApp, Google, Chanlytics, CSV, QR code.
- **No full stop** on a button, badge, heading, toast or a line under a name. Sentences in a notice or a Why end with one.
- **" · "** joins facts on one line, three facts at most. **No semicolons. No em dashes:** a dash is a new line or a " · ".
- **"…"** only for something under way: "Sending…".
- **Numbers as digits:** "3 booked", "$40", "1,250 people". A count says its noun in the right form: "1 person", "2 people".
- **Times and days** from the house's time words (they wear `SAYS_WHEN`): "2:30 PM", "Thu", "Nov 5", "1h 30m".
- **The owner's own words** in curly quotes: “Can we do Monday at 9?”.
- **Room for longer words:** a name, an appointment word or a later language can be longer than the English draft.
- **No word tables per language** on a screen. Words AI can say, AI says in the person's language; a screen's own words
  are one language until an app-wide decision says otherwise.

## The look of small words

Four styles for any words beside or under the main text, and nothing else on a page:

| Style   | Size        | Colour                               | For                                  |
| ------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Meta    | `--text-xs` | `--ink-dim`                          | The line under a name, times, counts |
| Help    | `--text-sm` | `--ink-dim`                          | A setting's one line on what it does |
| Warning | `--text-sm` | `--warn-ink`, with the alert glyph   | Needs the owner soon                 |
| Problem | `--text-sm` | `--danger-ink`, with the alert glyph | Didn't happen                        |

`--ink-faint` is for placeholders and what is turned off, never for words a person must read. No pixel sizes.

## Nothing jumps

- Words a press brings arrive over the page (a toast) or in the place of what was pressed (Confirm does this). They never
  push the page down.
- A notice that can be there when a page opens is there in the first frame, or keeps its room until it is.
- A loading outline is the height of what replaces it, and never shows rows or numbers that are not there.
- A badge or a button whose words can change has room for the longest of them, at every width the app supports.
- Proved in a browser, not by eye: a page opened cold, with a long list, at a phone's width and a desk's, in Safari on
  an iPhone too.

## How it is held

Six measures in `check:debt`, the house's ratchet (`tests/repo/words/_words.mjs`, read from the syntax tree, never from
the text), each a row of [debt.md](debt.md). They take only what a person reads: the words in the markup, the labels,
the words files and view models. An id, a key, a class, a thrown Error's text and a log line are not words, and
neither is what is said to a model. Who reads a file is said once, by where it lives (`AUDIENCES`, the table at the
top of this page):

| Measure          | Reads                                     | Counts                                                                                               |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `screen-words`   | The pages and contracts' words            | The words that never reach a screen, by family: AI's name, the code's, a provider's, retired, failed |
| `screen-voice`   | The same                                  | "we", an apology, "click", an em dash, a semicolon, a "Your" label, a long line, three sentences     |
| `server-words`   | A refusal's sentence, an answer's message | The same families                                                                                    |
| `taught-words`   | AI's instructions and its tools' words    | AI's name, the code's, a provider's, retired                                                         |
| `customer-words` | The pages Chanlytics hosts for a business | The code's and a provider's                                                                          |
| `title-tooltips` | The markup                                | Each `title=` on an element                                                                          |

The list itself is `tests/repo/words/_screen-words.mjs`, and holds only the words that mean one thing: row, record,
run, job, policy, line and their kind are English too, so a person reads them in each page's pass. Each count may only
fall; a change that raises one fails unless the pull request argues it (`Debt: <why>`), and a line that must keep a
word says why beside it, `screen-word: <why>`, on the line, over it, or over the declaration it stands in. One count
is at zero and held outright (`tests/repo/words/words.test.mjs`): outside the demo's sample screens, no page calls AI
by another name. `tests/repo/trade-neutral.test.mjs` stays as it is. Where a word decides what the product does (an
offer, a consent), a test runs that behaviour; the count does not stand in for it.
