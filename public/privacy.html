Privacy Policy

Chanlytics Messaging · Operated by RJ Capital LLC · Last updated: August 26, 2026

Chanlytics Messaging ("we", "our") provides WhatsApp Business Platform messaging services to small-business clients (such as beauty salons) as a Meta Tech Provider. This policy describes how we handle data when providing those services.

Data we process

On behalf of our business clients, we process: WhatsApp messages sent to and from the client's business phone number (message content, sender phone number, timestamps); business account metadata (WhatsApp Business Account settings, message templates, phone number configuration); and message delivery events provided by Meta's webhooks.

How we use it

Message data is used solely to operate messaging services for the business the customer contacted — including automated (AI-assisted) replies to customer inquiries, routing conversations to human staff, and providing the business with conversation summaries. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than the service providers required to operate the service (Meta Platforms, cloud hosting, and the AI model provider processing message text to generate replies).

Automated responses

Some replies are generated by an AI assistant acting on behalf of the business. Customers can request a human at any time and the conversation is routed to business staff.

Retention & deletion

Message logs are retained only as long as needed to provide the service to the business client. To request access to or deletion of your data, contact us or the business you messaged, and we will act on verified requests within 30 days.

Security

Webhook traffic is verified with Meta's cryptographic signatures; credentials are stored server-side in access-controlled environments; data is transmitted over HTTPS.
