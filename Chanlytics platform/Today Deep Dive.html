Today — the operator's brief

Composition: HeroBrief → AgentCommand + NowPlayingRail → SegmentPulse → AgentFleet → AmbientDockFiles: dashboard-a*.jsx · dashboard-data.jsx

Today is the surface that makes the promise of the product visible in ten seconds: what the agents did, what's about to happen, and the short list that needs a human. It's also the surface most likely to drift from the rails, because it re-presents state that lives elsewhere — and re-presentation is where second truths breed.

01What it gets right

The bones are correct — most of the system contracts already hold here.

  • One queue, two lenses. Command rows read/write IX_STORE by threadId; a decision here resolves in Inbox, on the schedule rail's risk chips, and in Tasks — and vice versa. The title row says so explicitly.
  • Engine + gate double-guard. decide() checks canDo("approve"), buttons spread window.gate("approve"), ⇧A respects both.
  • Live overlays everywhere. Fleet pauses (FL_STORE) reflect in the hero count, filter chips, and fleet cards; schedule risk chips flip to "Approved — confirmed" from IX records; the dock reacts to live:ended.
  • PolicyChips on rows (task.policyClass), deep links on every entity, computed section meta — no hardcoded counts in headers.

02Gaps found

Seven, ranked. The first two are contract violations; the rest are dead ends and blind spots.

03Deliberately fine as-is

  • KPI anchors by index (TODAY_KPIS[0,1,3]) — seed-coupled but honest; a backend sends a curated list.
  • AmbientDock's Selena coupling — scripted demo moment, already tied to LM_STORE correctly.
  • SegmentPulse — nav-only rows on the shared card grammar; nothing commits, nothing to gate.
  • Command's seed rows staying three — the point is triage theater with real rails, not a full Inbox mirror; #1's "+N more" chip closes the honesty gap without duplicating Inbox.

Verdict. Today's spine is sound — same queue, same stores, same gates. The two real violations are representation drift: a waiting count computed from the wrong source, and an autonomy meter speaking a vocabulary the rest of the system retired. Both fixes are deletions of second truths, not new machinery.

All seven are local to the three dashboard files + one seed field; zero rail changes. Say which to build.
